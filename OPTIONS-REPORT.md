# Phase 4: Paid Acquisition & Outreach Options Report
*Document ID: C4H-SLIM-4.7*

## 1. Google Ads (Search)
**Strategy:** Target high-intent queries (e.g., "sell house fast essex", "stop repossession").
- **Cost:** High CPC (£10-£25) due to national competitors (webuyanyhome, etc.).
- **Effort:** Medium. Requires strict negative keyword management and local radius targeting.
- **Compliance Risk:** Medium. Ads must strictly adhere to Google's financial products and real estate policies. Must not make guarantees without clear terms on the landing page.

## 2. Meta Lead Ads (Facebook/Instagram)
**Strategy:** Capture details in-app without requiring a website visit.
- **Cost:** Medium (£30-£60 CPL).
- **Effort:** Medium.
- **Compliance Risk:** **High**. Real estate and property buying fall under the **Special Ad Category** (Housing). This heavily restricts targeting (no age, gender, or zip code granularity; 15-mile radius minimum). Language must not imply discrimination or predatory financial practices.

## 3. Community Groups (Facebook/Nextdoor)
**Strategy:** Authentic engagement in local Essex community groups (not spamming, but offering value/advice).
- **Cost:** Zero (Time only).
- **Effort:** High. Requires the "Help-First" approach to build trust over time.
- **Compliance Risk:** Low, but reputational risk is high if perceived as a "shark". Must strictly follow the empathy guidelines.

## 4. Local Referral Partners
**Strategy:** Partner with local solicitors, mortgage brokers, and debt advice charities.
- **Cost:** Referral fees (percentage or flat fee).
- **Effort:** High upfront networking effort.
- **Compliance Risk:** Medium. Must ensure referral fee transparency under CPUTR and estate agency regulations.

## 5. Town x Segment Landing Pages
**Strategy:** Create highly specific SEO landing pages (e.g., "Stop Repossession Southend").
- **Cost:** Low (Development time).
- **Effort:** High content generation.
- **Compliance Risk:** Low, provided the Hive Mind Compliance Sentinel checks the copy for "guaranteed full market value" or "act now" pressure tactics.
