Our Story

Founded by Andrew Stallard, Cash 4 Houses was established to provide an alternative, stress-free route for homeowners looking to sell quickly in the South East Essex area. We are not a faceless national corporation; we are local experts who understand the true value of property in Southend, Basildon, and the surrounding areas.

Over the years, we've helped hundreds of homeowners navigate difficult situations—from probate sales to financial pressures and broken chains. Our core philosophy is simple: absolute transparency, rapid execution, and an empathetic approach to every seller's unique circumstances.
